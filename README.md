# Revaive

**An Open-Source Autonomous Business Growth Operating System.**

Revaive is a model-agnostic, self-hostable AI-powered business operating system designed to understand, predict, optimize and improve business performance.

It continuously analyzes business data, identifies growth opportunities, predicts potential outcomes, develops strategies and executes approved actions across sales, marketing, advertising and payment operations.

Unlike traditional AI assistants that primarily respond to prompts, Revaive is designed around continuous business intelligence, controlled autonomy and measurable outcomes.

## Vision

Build an intelligent business operating system that doesn't simply report what is happening, but understands why it is happening, predicts what may happen next and determines what should be done.

**Observe → Understand → Predict → Strategize → Execute → Evaluate → Learn → Improve**

## Core Capabilities

### 1. Business Intelligence
- Unified business data analysis.
- Revenue and profitability monitoring.
- Performance analysis.
- Opportunity and anomaly detection.

### 2. Predictive Intelligence
- Revenue and sales forecasting.
- Customer churn prediction.
- Demand forecasting.
- Conversion prediction.
- Financial and operational risk detection.

### 3. Autonomous Growth
- Sales optimization.
- Marketing strategy generation.
- Customer retention strategies.
- Growth opportunity discovery.
- Controlled experimentation.

### 4. Advertising Operations
- Advertising performance analysis.
- Campaign creation and management.
- Budget optimization.
- Audience and creative experimentation.
- Campaign performance monitoring.

### 5. Payment Intelligence & Operations
- Transaction analysis.
- Payment failure detection.
- Refund and subscription workflows.
- Payment reconciliation.
- Authorized payment operations.

### 6. Cognitive Learning
- Persistent business memory.
- Experiment outcome tracking.
- Strategy adaptation.
- Reflection and evidence-based learning.

### 7. Safe Self-Improvement
- Detect implementation inefficiencies.
- Identify recurring system limitations.
- Propose engineering improvements when new validated knowledge warrants them.
- Test generated code in isolated environments.
- Require approval before production deployment.

**Code generation is not the learning mechanism.** Business insights should primarily update structured memory, policies and strategies. Code changes are reserved for validated engineering improvements.

## Deployment Philosophy

Revaive is designed to support different deployment and inference configurations.

- Self-hosted infrastructure.
- Cloud deployment.
- Local AI inference.
- Bring Your Own Key (BYOK).
- Private model endpoints.
- Hybrid inference.

Users choose their deployment environment, model providers and data-processing configuration.

Application hosting and model inference are independent choices.

## Core Principles

1. Open-source first.
2. Model and provider agnostic.
3. User-controlled deployment and data.
4. Progressive autonomy.
5. Token-efficient execution.
6. Deterministic business logic wherever possible.
7. Evidence-driven decisions.
8. Financial and operational safety.
9. Minimal, useful cognitive learning.
10. Controlled and validated self-improvement.

## Autonomy Philosophy

Revaive begins by observing and analyzing business operations.

It progressively gains execution permissions according to user-defined policies, verified capabilities and approval requirements.

Financial operations, advertising budgets and other sensitive actions must remain within explicit authorization boundaries.

## Development Status

**Status: Architecture and system design**

The project is currently in its documentation and foundational design stage. Implementation decisions will be finalized through the architecture and system design process.

## Long-Term Vision

Revaive aims to become an extensible, autonomous business operating system capable of coordinating business intelligence, predictions, sales, advertising, payments and continuous improvement through a unified agent runtime.
